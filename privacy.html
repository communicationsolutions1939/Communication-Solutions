Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly.

Last Updated: September 2026

Communication Solutions ("Communication Solutions," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information provided through our website and related services.

This policy applies to information collected through the Communication Solutions website, including information submitted through our contact, employer inquiry, and job application forms.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City or location
  • Employment and professional information
  • Information about positions you are interested in
  • Information about your hiring needs
  • Messages and other information you choose to provide

We only ask for information that is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is collected.

Information Submitted Through Our Forms

Our website contains forms that allow visitors to contact us, inquire about recruiting services, or submit information for employment opportunities.

When you submit information through one of these forms, the information you provide may be processed and stored by third-party service providers that help us operate our website and receive form submissions.

Our current forms use Formspree to process form submissions. Information submitted through our forms may therefore be transmitted to and stored by Formspree in accordance with its own privacy practices and terms.

How We Use Your Information

We may use information we collect to:

  • Respond to questions and inquiries
  • Communicate with employers about recruiting services
  • Evaluate individuals for employment opportunities
  • Communicate with job seekers regarding opportunities
  • Coordinate recruiting and hiring activities
  • Operate and maintain our website
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse of our website
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

Job Applicant Information

If you submit information through our job application process, we may use that information to evaluate your qualifications for current or future employment opportunities and to communicate with you regarding those opportunities.

Information submitted as part of an application may be shared with an employer when reasonably necessary to evaluate you for a position or facilitate the recruiting process.

Submitting an application does not guarantee an interview, employment, or placement with an employer.

Employer Information

Employers may provide information about their company, hiring needs, open positions, contact information, and other recruiting requirements.

We use this information to communicate with employers, understand their hiring needs, provide recruiting services, and facilitate candidate searches and hiring activities.

How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide recruiting services, including:

  • With employers when necessary to facilitate recruiting or evaluate a candidate for a position
  • With service providers that help us operate our website or process information
  • When required by law or legal process
  • When necessary to protect our rights, property, users, or business

Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party services to help operate our website and business. These services may process information on our behalf.

Our current website uses Formspree to process online form submissions. Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies governing information they receive or process.

Website Hosting

Our website is currently hosted through GitHub Pages. Information related to website requests may be processed by the hosting provider as part of operating and securing the website.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, security, or performance.

We do not currently use the website to sell personal information or to operate a targeted advertising program.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through our website from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, provide recruiting services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or otherwise operate our business.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

These rights may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable legal exceptions and requirements.

If you would like to make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

Communication Solutions operates in Texas. Texas residents may have privacy rights under applicable Texas law, including rights concerning access to, correction of, and deletion of certain personal information, as well as certain rights regarding the use of personal information.

Privacy rights and applicable exemptions can vary depending on the circumstances and the type of information involved. We will review and respond to valid privacy requests in accordance with applicable law.

If we become aware that we have unintentionally collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take reasonable steps to delete the information.
