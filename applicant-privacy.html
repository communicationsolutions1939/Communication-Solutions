Applicant Privacy Notice

How we handle information submitted during the recruiting and application process.

Last Updated: September 2026

Communication Solutions ("Communication Solutions," "we," "our," or "us") respects the privacy of individuals who provide information to us while seeking employment opportunities.

This Applicant Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and share information provided by individuals who apply for positions or otherwise participate in our recruiting process.

This notice supplements our Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

When you apply for a position or communicate with us about employment opportunities, we may collect information such as:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City or location
  • Employment history and experience
  • Information about positions you are interested in
  • Information you provide about your qualifications
  • Messages or additional information you choose to provide

The information we request may vary depending on the position and stage of the recruiting process.

How We Use Applicant Information

We may use information you provide to:

  • Review your qualifications for employment opportunities
  • Match you with positions that may be appropriate for your experience and qualifications
  • Communicate with you regarding available positions
  • Coordinate interviews and other recruiting activities
  • Provide your information to an employer when appropriate for consideration for a position
  • Maintain recruiting and business records
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

Sharing Applicant Information With Employers

Communication Solutions provides direct-hire recruiting services to employers.

When appropriate, we may provide information you submit to an employer for the purpose of evaluating your qualifications for a position.

We will generally consider the requirements of the position, the information you provide, and the recruiting process when determining whether your information should be presented to an employer.

Once information has been provided to an employer, that employer may handle the information according to its own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

Current and Future Opportunities

Information you provide may be considered for the specific position for which you applied as well as other employment opportunities that may reasonably match your qualifications and experience.

If we believe another opportunity may be a better fit, we may contact you about that opportunity.

Information Submitted Through Our Application Form

Our online application form is currently processed through Formspree.

Information submitted through the application form may therefore be transmitted to and stored by Formspree in accordance with its applicable policies and terms.

We recommend reviewing the privacy practices of any third-party service used to process information submitted through our website.

Accuracy of Your Information

We ask applicants to provide accurate and truthful information.

Providing inaccurate, misleading, or incomplete information may affect our ability to evaluate your application or communicate with you about employment opportunities.

Data Retention

We may retain applicant information for as long as reasonably necessary for recruiting purposes, consideration for future opportunities, business recordkeeping, dispute resolution, or compliance with applicable legal requirements.

The amount of time information is retained may vary depending on the circumstances and applicable requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information you provide to us.

These rights may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and exceptions.

If you would like to make a privacy-related request regarding information you have submitted to Communication Solutions, please contact us using the information below.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect applicant information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

No Guarantee of Employment

Submitting an application or providing information to Communication Solutions does not guarantee an interview, job offer, employment, or placement with an employer.

Employment decisions are made by the applicable employer based on its own hiring requirements and processes.

Changes to This Notice

We may update this Applicant Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect changes to our recruiting practices, website, services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this notice.
