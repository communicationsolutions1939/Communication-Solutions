For Employers

Recruiting solutions built around your business.

Finding the right people is essential to keeping your business moving forward. Communication Solutions works alongside employers to understand their staffing needs, identify qualified candidates, and support the hiring process from the initial search through the final hiring decision.

Whether you're filling an individual position, managing ongoing hiring needs, or expanding your workforce, we provide flexible recruiting support tailored to your organization. Our Process

From First Conversation
To the Right Hire

A clear, coordinated approach to moving your hiring needs forward, from the initial discussion through the final stages of the hiring process.

GETTING STARTED

Initial Conversation

We begin by learning about your organization, current hiring needs, and expectations for the position. This is an opportunity to discuss your goals, timelines, and any challenges you may be experiencing.

01
DEFINING THE SEARCH

Establishing the Search

Together, we establish the details of the position, including qualifications, responsibilities, compensation expectations, and other considerations that will guide the recruiting effort.

02
FINDING TALENT

Candidate Introductions

As potential matches are identified, we introduce candidates for your consideration. You can review their experience, discuss their qualifications, and decide who should move forward.

03
MOVING FORWARD

Interview Coordination

We help coordinate communication between your team and selected candidates, including interview scheduling, feedback, and updates to keep the process moving.

04
MAKING THE DECISION

Hiring Decision

Once you've identified the right candidate, we help coordinate the final stages of the process, including offer discussions, outstanding questions, and the transition toward an accepted offer.

05
BEYOND THE OFFER

Placement Follow-Up

Our involvement doesn't have to end when an offer is accepted. We remain available to check in, address outstanding concerns, and help the placement get off to a positive start.
